# Examples

- [Threshold](/examples/threshold/) — how far a drag must go before the slide
  snaps.
- [Transition](/examples/transition/) — controlling the snap animation speed.
- [Scale on drag](/examples/scale-on-drag/) — the slide scales while you drag.
- [Controlled with buttons](/examples/controlled/) — driving `activeIndex`
  from your own state with prev/next buttons.
- [Callbacks](/examples/callbacks/) — watching `onSlideStart` and
  `onSlideComplete`.
- [Keyboard](/examples/keyboard/) — navigating with the arrow keys.
